Starling Sky

At dusk, thousands of starlings move as one cloud. Each bird follows three simple rules. Change them and watch the sky change.

The three rules

  • Cohesion: steer toward the centre of nearby birds.
  • Alignment: steer toward the average direction of nearby birds.
  • Separation: steer away from birds that are too close.

No bird is in charge. The swirling shapes come from every bird reacting only to its neighbours. Click the sky to make a gust that scatters them.
